# Complete D&D Book & Supplement Catalog for Character Building

This document catalogs the complete library of sourcebooks, player packs, and supplements owned and available to the user. When building characters, proposing options, or analyzing theorycrafting synergies, cross-reference these sources for **classes, subclasses, species/lineages, background feats, spells, magic items, and unique systems**.

## 🏛️ Official D&D / Wizards of the Coast (WotC)

### 1. Player’s Handbook (2024) (PHB 2024)
- **Status:** Primary Ruleset & Source of Truth.
- **Classes (12):** Barbarian, Bard, Cleric, Druid, Fighter, Monk, Paladin, Ranger, Rogue, Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard (48 total subclasses, all unlocked at Level 3).
- **Species:** Aasimar, Dragonborn, Dwarf, Elf, Gnome, Goliath, Halfling, Human, Orc, Tiefling.
- **Feats:** 
  - *Origin Feats (Level 1 via Background):* Alert, Crafter, Healer, Lucky, Magic Initiate, Musician, Savage Attacker, Skilled, Tavern Brawler, Tough.
  - *General Feats (Level 4+):* Feats with stat bumps (+1 ASI), fighting style feats, masteries.
  - *Epic Boon Feats (Level 19+).*
- **Core Mechanics:** Weapon Masteries (Cleave, Graze, Nick, Push, Sap, Slow, Topple, Vex), updated spell action economy (1 spell slot per turn), reworked spells (Divine Smite as BA spell, revised Healing Word, Counterspell CON save).

## 🛠️ Harmonization & 2024 Rules Adaptation Engine

When mixing third-party or legacy material with D&D 2024 (5.5E), adhere to these principles:

1. **Subclass Acquisition at Level 3:** Any subclass (even if the original 2014 source granted it at Level 1 or 2, such as Cleric domains or Sorcerer origins) is acquired at **Level 3**.
2. **Backgrounds & Ability Scores:** All characters receive **+2/+1 or +1/+1/+1** to ability scores via their Background (not from Species). Each background should also grant an **Origin Feat**.
3. **Weapon Masteries:** Martial classes and subclasses that rely on weapons can utilize 2024 Weapon Masteries (including third-party trick weapons and firearms where applicable).
4. **Spell Bonus Action Economy:** Casting a spell with a Bonus Action limits any other spells cast that turn to cantrips with a casting time of 1 action (2024 rules).
5. **Class Identity Preservation:** Unique classes (Pugilist, Illrigger, Gunslinger, Warcraft classes) retain their signature resource pools (Moxie, Interdict Seals, Deed Points, Mana/Fury) while adhering to 2024 action/bonus action/reaction economy.
