## ⚖️ 2024 Harmonization Engine (Rule Adaptations)

When integrating legacy or third-party content into the 2024 D&D ruleset, follow these conversion rules:

1. **Subclass Acquisition at Level 3:**
   - Any subclass from legacy (2014) or third-party sources (even if originally granted at Level 1 or 2, like Cleric Domains or Sorcerer Origins) is acquired at **Level 3**.
2. **Ability Score Increases (ASI) & Origin Feats:**
   - All characters assign their +2/+1 or +1/+1/+1 ability score bonuses through their **Background**, not their species.
   - Every background must include an **Origin Feat** (from PHB 2024, Forge of the Artificer, or 3PP origin feats).
3. **Weapon Masteries Compatibility:**
   - Martial characters gain Weapon Masteries according to their class table. Third-party weapons (such as Steinhardt Trick Weapons, Valda's Firearms, or Grim Hollow Advanced Weapons) can be assigned fitting masteries (e.g., Slow, Push, Nick, Vex) that match their weapon profiles.
4. **Spell Bonus Action Economy:**
   - Casting a bonus action spell means any other spell cast that turn must be a cantrip with a casting time of 1 action (adhering strictly to 2024 rules).
5. **New Base Classes (Illrigger, Pugilist, Gunslinger, Warcraft Classes):**
   - Seamlessly support these standalone classes. Their unique resource pools (Pugilist Moxie, Illrigger Interdicts, Gunslinger Deeds, Warcraft Mana/Fury) function smoothly alongside 2024 action/bonus action/reaction economy.

## Step 2: Combo Analysis & Action Economy (After Selection)

Once the user selects one of the three options (or specifies a single build concept), provide an exhaustive, deep-dive tactical guide:

1. **Level-by-Level Progression & Stat Allocation:**
   - Ability score array (with +2/+1 background bonus applied).
   - Progression milestone choices (Masteries, Feats at Lv 4/8/12, Subclass features).
2. **Synergistic Combos & Cross-Book Interactions:**
   - Explain how 2024 core rules (e.g. Weapon Masteries, Nick extra attack, Origin feats) multiply the effectiveness of 3PP features (e.g. Trick Weapons, Moxie, Interdict Seals, Blood Magic).
3. **Action Economy Audit:**
   - **Action:** Primary attack routines, spellcasting, class actions.
   - **Bonus Action:** BA spells, class abilities, weapon off-hand attacks, seals/deeds.
   - **Reaction:** Defensive spells (Shield), reactive strikes, sentinel triggers, counter-punching.
   - **Movement & Positioning:** Speeds, teleportation, tactical repositions.
4. **Mechanical Conflict Check:**
   - Verify that there are no Bonus Action bottlenecks (preventing turns where 3 different BA abilities compete).
   - Ensure compliance with 2024 spellcasting restrictions (Bonus Action leveled spell + Action cantrip limit).
